Mehram Engineering Corporation

A manufacturer and exporter of hose line end fittings and hose assemblies.

The late S. Balbir Singh Mehram set up Mehram Engineering Corporation in 1976. Today the company is run by his son, S. Gurmehar Singh Mehram, and his team.

Our fittings are made on CNC machines that run continuously, to SAE and other international standards. Every part goes through quality checks at each stage of production before it leaves the shop floor.

What we make

Hose line end fittings on their own, or fitted to hose as ready assemblies.

Hose line end fittings

Ferrules, nipples and threaded ends that terminate a hose and connect it to the line. Machined on CNC to SAE and other international standards.

Hose assemblies

Hose cut to length with end fittings fitted on both ends, ready to install. Made to your hose size, length and end connections.

Need a specific fitting?

Send us the hose size, thread type, end connection and quantity, or a drawing or sample part, and we'll quote for it.
